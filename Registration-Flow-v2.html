Member Registration Flow — v2 (draft)

Working brainstorm — not published to frd/05-members.md

Revises the uploaded flow to a three-way age split (Child 0-12 / Teen 13-17 / Adult 18+), adds the Child email-matching branch, an explicit GUARDIAN_APPROVED status, guardian-approval expiry/escalation, and rejected_by tracking. Full reasoning in Registration-Status-Flow-Brainstorm.md.
